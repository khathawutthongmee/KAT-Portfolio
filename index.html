ABOUT

The world through
a pocket lens.

For those who see stories in shadows — My Name is Khathawut, a Bangkok-based photographer who has spent over eight years proving that the most powerful camera is the one you always carry.

My work spans the raw energy of street photography, the quiet geometry of minimalist architecture, the intimacy of documentary portraiture, and the expansiveness of travel. Every image is captured on iPhone — not as a limitation, but as a philosophy: presence over equipment.

My post-production workflow is built entirely in Adobe Lightroom, where I have developed a signature tonal language — high-contrast monochrome with precise shadow recovery, custom grain profiles, and a restrained palette that references the silver gelatin prints of the darkroom era.
